# CRYPTO LAB — DIAMOND DNA PRECHECK V0.2

Use after the Negative Edge Map anti-duplication check and **before** opening protected outcomes for a new lab.

## A. Economic primitive
1. WHO PAYS / ACTS?
2. Is the actor forced, structurally incentivized, informed, hedging, rebalancing or merely correlated?
3. What direct observable measures that pressure?

## B. Causal timing
4. Why should the information not already be fully absorbed at T0?
5. Why is the frozen horizon the natural decay/response horizon?
6. What exact event/state ends the mechanism?

## C. Source
7. Is the source point-in-time, reproducible and immutable enough for the claim?
8. Can source quality be proven before outcomes?
9. Are there source gaps that would bias event inclusion?

## D. Economics
10. What is the realistic base friction?
11. What is the stress friction?
12. After Discovery only: does observed effect size have meaningful headroom over both?
13. If the expected effect is sub-bps, is this an execution overlay rather than standalone alpha?

## E. Validation
14. Is a genuinely untouched OOS/replication block available?
15. Is the minimum sample mechanism-aware rather than arbitrary?
16. What concentration/tail test will prevent one-event promotion?

## F. Execution
17. Can historical/prospective quotes, depth, size or fill proxies be obtained without changing the signal?
18. Is the target notional executable under the frozen assumptions?
19. What would fail closed operationally?

## G. Adversarial explanation
20. What placebo/matched-control explanation could reproduce the pattern?
21. Pre-positioning?
22. Regime/volatility?
23. Asset beta / market drift?
24. Selection effect?
25. Data publication delay?

## Routing heuristic
- Strong, source-ready answers across all sections: PRIORITY_A
- Mechanism strong but source/execution not ready: PRIORITY_B_SOURCE_FIRST
- Economics likely too small for friction: COST_HEADROOM_FAIL
- No independent evidence path: REPLICATION_PATH_MISSING
- No identifiable pressure / mostly cosmetic price transform: LOW_INFORMATION_NOVELTY
- Overlaps MECHANISM_EXHAUSTED without a new primitive: DUPLICATE_COVERED

This is a research-routing heuristic only. It does not promote, authorize capital or replace frozen candidate-specific gates.
