## 5. The actual survivor signature

The strongest common pattern is not simply “external information beats price.”

The better description is:

> **A useful edge candidate tends to measure a real market pressure, at the time-scale on which that pressure acts, with enough economic magnitude to survive costs, and then survive genuinely independent evidence.**

Seven recurring properties:

1. **Named payer / constrained actor**
   - utility demand, institutional positioning, hedging/arbitrage, liquidity replenishment, forced deleveraging, borrower stress.
   - “price went up therefore it may keep going up” is a much weaker causal primitive.

2. **Direct state variable**
   - official event timestamp/utility, CFTC positioning, option/spot-perp state, L2 depth/replenishment, borrower health factor, protocol accounting.
   - indirect proxies repeatedly create descriptive-but-not-predictive results.

3. **Mechanism-matched horizon**
   - L2 acts in seconds/minutes.
   - U.S.-open volatility acts in tens of minutes.
   - institutional positioning acts over days/weeks.
   - Launchpool demand was tested over a 24h event window.
   - AVAX trend uses a much slower 20D state.
   Arbitrary horizon sweeps are not equivalent to causal matching.

4. **Friction headroom**
   - BNB has large economic room.
   - ETF, OPTIONS and CED survive base costs but are thin/fragile.
   - L2 proves the counterexample: very strong statistical consistency with an effect too small for standard execution.

5. **Independent evidence**
   Every current Tier-2 candidate obtained materially independent evidence: disjoint blocks or untouched/OOS confirmation.
   Attractive Discovery alone has repeatedly produced stones.

6. **Execution/capacity evidence**
   A model without fill/quote/capacity evidence is unfinished.
   CED's bookDepth corroboration is a good example; BTC-VRP is the opposite example.

7. **Adversarial alternative-explanation attack**
   BNB's pre-positioning and volatility diagnostics show why a profitable pattern is not automatically a clean causal story.
   A Diamond needs to survive the explanation attack, not only the PnL test.

## 6. Failure mechanisms that this signature explains

### Price-only technical mining
Usually has no identifiable payer, often uses many interchangeable horizons/thresholds, and tends to show low headroom over costs. This explains the Pattern/Scalping/indicator cemetery.

### Generic flow
Flow often measures what is happening **now**, not what must happen next. ETF-CME differs because the state is slower and tied to institutional positioning; generic taker/shortflow/cross-chain mappings often failed.

### Macro-event labels
“CPI happened” or even simple signed surprise is too coarse. A causal state needs point-in-time expectation, TradFi repricing and transmission state.

### Mean reversion with weak anchor
Mean reversion is compelling only when there is an executable economic anchor and sufficient event density. A peg/NAV story without enough events or with friction larger than the dislocation is not an edge.

### Microstructure
Microstructure can be scientifically real but economically microscopic. Use it only when:
- friction is low enough, or
- it improves execution/timing of an already-valid parent edge,
not simply because a bootstrap is beautiful.

## 7. Positive search map — where future research has the best scientific rationale

This is a research-priority map, **not a trading recommendation or edge claim**.

### PRIORITY A — constrained / forced / mandatory flow with direct state
Examples of unresolved primitives:
- liquidation eligibility / oracle-distance convexity
- borrower health-factor crowding
- actual realized liquidation flow
- redemption/mint/burn states with direct protocol accounting
Why: the actor may be forced or structurally constrained, not merely predictive by correlation.

### PRIORITY A — executable relative value with direct anchors
Examples:
- option/perp/spot dislocations
- protocol NAV vs executable market when direct liquid venue exists
- naturally hedged basis where point-in-time source and costs are provable
Why: payoff is tied to convergence/hedging rather than a naked direction guess.

### PRIORITY A/B — slow institutional / positioning flow
Examples:
- exact point-in-time ETF/CME/institutional inventory changes
Why: slow information decay can leave enough time for execution even when per-trade edge is thin.

### PRIORITY B — volatility state with monetization source solved first
Examples:
- clock-conditioned vol
- HAR/weekday realized-vol state
- VRP/term-structure
Rule: prove historical/executable quote source **before** spending a protected outcome on an economic translation.

### PRIORITY B — event demand with pre-positioning explicitly modeled
BNB proves event utility can be powerful, but the pre-event drift challenge is too large to ignore.
Future event labs should freeze:
- T-24h / T-12h / T0 pre-positioning diagnostics
- matched momentum controls
- matched volatility/regime controls
before causal claims.

### PRIORITY C — pure price persistence
Allowed only when:
- horizon is economically defensible,
- the rule is simple/frozen,
- gross edge has substantial cost headroom,
- untouched independent block exists,
- no asset/timeframe winner selection occurs.
AVAX20 proves it can survive; TFG parent and DH02 prove how fragile it can be.

## 8. Diamond DNA preflight

Before opening a new lab, answer all nine:

1. **WHO PAYS?** Which actor is forced, incentivized, informed, hedging or rebalancing?
2. **WHAT STATE?** What directly observable variable measures that pressure before the outcome?
3. **WHY NOW?** Why should the effect exist at this exact time rather than generically?
4. **WHY THIS HORIZON?** What mechanism determines decay/holding period?
5. **POINT-IN-TIME?** Can the source be reconstructed without hindsight?
6. **HEADROOM?** After Discovery, is the observed effect materially larger than realistic base/stress friction?
7. **INDEPENDENCE?** Is there genuinely untouched evidence available?
8. **EXECUTABLE?** Can quote/depth/fill/capacity evidence be obtained without changing the hypothesis?
9. **RED-TEAM ATTACK?** What non-edge explanation would make the same pattern appear?

Routing:
- 8–9 strong answers -> PRIORITY_A research candidate
- 6–7 -> PRIORITY_B / source or mechanism work first
- <=5 -> do not spend protected outcomes
- any overlap with MECHANISM_EXHAUSTED -> reject unless the new information primitive is materially different

The answer count is a routing heuristic, not a scientific score or promotion criterion.

## 9. New operating principle

Old question:
> Which indicator / pattern / timeframe should we test?

New question:
> **Which economic pressure is measurable point-in-time, why has it not already been fully absorbed, how long should it persist, and can its magnitude pay the real execution path?**

Only after that do we choose the statistical model.

## 10. Bottom line

The cemetery and the survivors together imply a sharper hypothesis:

> **The scarce asset is not a clever signal. It is a measurable economic constraint with enough persistence and magnitude to survive information decay, costs and independent validation.**

That shifts the Crypto Lab from **pattern mining** toward **pressure mining**.

No new market outcome was opened to produce this map.
