## Reopening test
If overlap exists, state the **materially new information**. Cosmetic changes do not qualify.

Acceptable distinctions:
- new economic mechanism
- new causal information primitive
- genuinely different market structure / venue
- direct executable relative-value geometry
- new source that measures a previously unobserved state

Insufficient distinctions:
- threshold change
- indicator parameter change
- favorable asset subset
- favorable year / subperiod
- lower assumed fees
- direction inversion after seeing outcomes
- cosmetic timeframe change
