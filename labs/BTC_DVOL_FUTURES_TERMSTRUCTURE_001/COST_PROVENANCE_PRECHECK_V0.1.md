   https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944746248989-Fees

## HISTORICAL PROVENANCE LIMIT

The launch announcement establishes the fee-family linkage to USDC instruments in March 2023, but the currently retrieved fee page is not itself proof of the exact standard-account trading fee on every day in 2023-2024. Therefore no historical after-cost claim may assume the 2026 trading rate as historical fact.

Before an execution MVE can claim historical after-cost expectancy, one of the following must be prospectively frozen:
- exact point-in-time historical Deribit fee provenance for the tested dates; or
- a documented conservative fee stress whose conservatism versus the historical standard fee can itself be established.

The 0.05% DVOL delivery fee is documented by the current official fee page, but historical continuity across the full 2023-2024 sample must still be adjudicated before it is treated as exact historical cost.
