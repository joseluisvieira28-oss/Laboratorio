# TOKEN-MIGRATION-FORCED-CONVERSION-BASIS-001
## V0.1 SOURCE GATE CLOSEOUT
Date: 2026-10-06

### Verdict
SOURCE_BLOCKED

### Why this is the legitimate verdict
The frozen gate requires >=12 independent migration programmes that are completely defensible BEFORE any market outcome is opened.

The source-only attack produced >=21 raw candidate programmes, so this is NOT a lack of ideas. However, fewer than 12 can currently satisfy all frozen requirements simultaneously:
- deterministic OLD -> NEW conversion economics;
- hard / immutable economic end boundary;
- identifiable retirement/deprecation consequence;
- migration/redemption mechanism at the required public-provenance standard;
- activation transaction/block or equivalent immutable activation receipt;
- unambiguous OLD/NEW identity;
- per-event ability to obtain contemporaneous historical OLD and NEW market observations under a venue/quote policy frozen before outcomes;
- no outcome-based selection.

Several otherwise attractive migrations are explicitly open-ended and therefore fail the frozen end-boundary rule. Several exchange-supported swaps provide ratio and service-cutoff evidence but do not prove a global migration contract, activation block, or a contemporaneous OLD+NEW market on the same venue. Several hard-deadline project migrations remain only provisional because the exact migrator / activation receipt and outcome-free historical market-data coverage have not been established.

A complete global universe of qualifying 2022-2025 forced/economically-binding migrations also cannot be demonstrated from an authoritative public registry. Therefore the shortfall cannot honestly be labeled INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE; doing so would require proving that the complete eligible universe contains fewer than 12. The frozen taxonomy dictates SOURCE_BLOCKED when coverage/provenance cannot be demonstrated.

### Scientific interpretation
The economic mechanism remains plausible enough to deserve future reconsideration if a defensible source frame becomes available, but the present public evidence package cannot support an unbiased >=12-event Development test under the frozen rules.

This closeout is a source/provenance failure, not evidence for or against convergence of conversion-adjusted basis.

### Reopening rule
Do not reopen this family by lowering gates.

A future reopening would require NEW source capability, for example:
1. a reproducible authoritative migration registry or mechanically enumerable source frame for 2022-2025;
2. public on-chain migrator/activation receipts for enough candidates;
3. event-specific, outcome-blind metadata proving contemporaneous historical OLD+NEW data availability for >=12 independent programmes.

If those source conditions are met, create a NEW source freeze/version. Do not modify this V0.1 closeout and do not inspect historical prices first.
