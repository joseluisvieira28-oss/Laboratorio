## Why this is economically different

**Who pays?** A trader or small bot operator who wants to know whether their trading system made money *after* commissions, funding, spreads, partial fills and accounting errors.

**What is being sold?** A fixed-price human-audited PDF summary with a source-linked net P&L reconciliation, cost decomposition, anomalies, execution-risk checklist and 3 prioritized remedies (where evidence permits). NOT a promise to make the customer profitable.

**Mechanism:** professional service revenue paid by the client, independent of market alpha; no proprietary balance sheet or leveraged exposure. Unique learning from prior Crypto Lab: fees and incomplete receipts can invert a gross P&L verdict. No claims that those earlier cases were third-party client outcomes.

**What would kill it?** No willing paying customers after structured outreach; recurring inability to reconcile usable source exports in two hours; or clients preferring cheap/free generic journals.

Disclose external dependencies, refunds scope and client confidentiality before collecting private records.

There is **no sales evidence yet**. A checkout page, API connection, or new executable would be premature and repeat the Crypto Lab failure mode. The next expensive step is forbidden until external demand appears. A manually delivered report is the lowest-cost empirical experiment.
