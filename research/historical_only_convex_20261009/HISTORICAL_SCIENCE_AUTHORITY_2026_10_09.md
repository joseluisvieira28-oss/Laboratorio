## Decision authority and scope
**Future-event waiting is NOT mandatory for a HISTORICAL scientific verdict.** Historically frozen hypotheses can receive historical PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE as soon as historical original datasets, accounting and independent data partitions pass the stated research gates. This is a new parallel research-decision framework on an isolated branch; historical authority is a **research grade**, not an exchange or real-capital trading license.
The original experiments' pre-existing scientific freezes, original outcome labels and sealed 2026 partitions remain unchanged. No new supposedly pristine historical OOS can be created from periods or symbols already opened and analyzed; replaying the same ledger is **reproducibility**, not independent OOS.
An unambiguously favorable historical source-tested mechanism is allowed to be labelled `HISTORICAL_SCIENCE_PASS_SCOPE_LIMITED` (with precise universe/time/venue), even without additional future observations. A strategy failing the next independent historical market/time segment receives FAIL, not rescued by asset/timeframe choice.

## Three separate questions
1. **Historical mechanism/ex-post simulation:** Can we independently reproduce complete date-covered original prices, event timestamp precedes trade, exact documented parameters, exchange fees, spread/slippage/funding, and a profitable after-cost distribution that survives pre-specified holdout/asset tests? State HISTORICAL_LOCAL_PASS, HISTORICAL_CROSSSECTION_PASS, HISTORICAL_FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE. A trade-ledger-only recalculation reports archived source verified, but cannot alone verify original bar sources/fill realism.
2. **Marketability/economic feasibility:** Would signals have had accessible marketable bid/ask/depth at order decision, legal venue access, realistic account-specific commission, funding, capital/margin, stop-gap tails? If unproven, label EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED. Do not convert public snapshots, 1h OPEN, proxy price, or theoretical no-arbitrage payoffs to actual orders.
3. **Live profitability:** Past returns cannot logically prove future returns; never call a mechanism an assured money printer. A new *live* risk authorization needs its own explicit operator mandate, size, loss controls, and regulatory/exchange feasibility. Nothing here authorizes orders or account reads.

## Historical-only classification without compulsory future waits
- Local historical research PASS: exact frozen original OOS/basket rules and cost model pass; record date, asset and risk. Never imply universality.
- General historical PASS: multiple independently selected historical time periods/assets and all pre-existing holdouts pass or have a predeclared proper scientifically scoped universe. Refuse cherry picking from already revealed negative instruments.
- General historical FAIL: an originally predeclared independent expansion/time gate fails, even if a local subset or one large-trend year wins.
- Source/ledger blocked: missing bars, fee tier, timestamp chronology, entry overlap, source artifact, fatal reconciliation. Technical/test PASS alone has zero economic credit.
- Historical uncertainty: small n, weak PF, large DD, results hinge on few trades, multiple-comparison uncorrected mining; remain scientifically weak despite positive point estimate.
