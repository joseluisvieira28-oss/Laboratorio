## Required evidence per independent event

Revisions/deferrals supersede originals only with official evidence. Missing/ambiguous timestamp, applicability or rule => reject/pending, never impute.
Independence unit is official policy announcement/deadline cluster, not number of tickers. Multiple amendments to one policy are one event. Minimum 12 independent defensible clusters with appropriate same-venue market-data source capability.
Public historical OI, mark/index or equivalent basis, and funding archives must be demonstrably available at event/control windows without reading numeric outcomes. HEAD/listing/checksum/schema-only checks permitted. Collateral/spot margin events lacking a defensible binding to affected derivative exposure do not count toward this OI discovery.
Require >=80% complete event coverage over ALL otherwise qualified clusters, >=12 covered independent clusters; per-event observations reported separately.
