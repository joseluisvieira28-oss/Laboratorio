## Timing / known_at and execution feasibility
For any genuinely new prospective study, record exchange event time, source publication time when provable, local request-start/receipt UTC and monotonic clock, sequence IDs, clock offset, reconnections and response bytes/hash. Define known_at as latest required receipt/availability time; historical event timestamp alone is not receipt-time proof. Signal uses only records known by decision, execution follows known_at plus nonnegative latency; exclude funding already paid. Archive publication/revisions need explicit caveats, including ms/us/ns normalization. Crossvenue max age/skew must be frozen before prices are viewed.

Entry/exit requires both legs' executable bid/ask and available size after latency. Midpoints, aggTrades and candle opens cannot prove fills; unmatched-leg risk, hedge precision, contract multiplier/lot/minimum size, queue priority, adverse selection, cancellations, outages and partial fills require explicit handling. No capital or capacity PASS is issued from a top-of-book lower bound.

Complete cost identity on a stated numeraire:
net = two-leg trading PnL + actual entitled funding - four fill fees - spread - size/latency slippage - borrow/locate/interest - transfer/network/withdrawal/FX - rebalancing and failed hedge costs - capital opportunity cost.
Report both single-leg-notional and total committed-capital returns. Long funded spot may avoid base borrow; it still consumes cash. Futures margin, stress buffer and liquidation risk remain. Pre-funded venues avoid per-trade transfers but require duplicated inventory and later rebalancing; never assume instant transfers. Unknown costs block promotion rather than default to zero. Applying extra nonnegative costs cannot rescue negative upper bounds.
