## Scientific interpretation
- The filtered wallet diversity **does not show any advantage** over a simple reserve gate in this observed comparison; its observed proxy mean is worse (-18.74% vs -9.65%). Group composition and conditional selection mean this is not a randomized causal comparison.
- This only tests a 2-hour window; no independent training/out-of-sample days, no full 30-day point-in-time stream, no receipts for actual user-visible quote latency, and no fills. Positive claims would be unwarranted even if a cherry-picked subgroup had positive proxy means.
- A detector requiring 30s latency and a fixed 5-minute sale may miss critical trading conditions; existing backend cannot certify an achievable quote.
- `curveSol`, `vs`, `vt` are reconstructed on-chain states; exact integer instruction-level fee program and real counterfactual user-impact are not recreated; cash-out may be impossible during migration, MEV, or rapid drain.
- October source data are licensed for **personal/research use within team only**; no raw sample was committed or uploaded. Any future commercial use requires checking terms.
- External evidence reinforces caution: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39458 prospectively finds team-following loses out of sample across 32 configurations.
