# BINANCE-DUAL-PRECURSOR-SPOT-HAZARD-003 — V0.1.4.1 PRE-OUTCOME SPOT SOURCE COMPLETENESS AMENDMENT
Date: 2026-10-06
Status: FROZEN BEFORE ANY SPOT OUTCOME CLASSIFICATION

## Reason
A title-only classifier of the form Binance Will List ... can miss an earlier official Binance article, such as a HODLer Airdrops, Launchpool or similar official product announcement, whose body explicitly announces the future Binance Spot listing.

Because the V0.1 endpoint is the first official Binance Spot-listing announcement timestamp, source completeness must be based on article content rather than title alone.

## Frozen remediation
For 2025 discovery, an official Binance CMS article qualifies as a Spot-listing announcement for token S when ALL are true:
1. official public Binance CMS article with defensible releaseDate;
2. article detail explicitly states that Binance will list / launch Spot trading for S;
3. article detail identifies S through an exact trading pair such as S/USDT or S/USDC, or an exact ticker/project identity in the listing statement;
4. the statement refers to Binance Spot, not only Alpha, Futures, Margin, Convert, Earn or another product.

The first qualifying releaseDate is SPOT_FIRST.

Candidate article metadata may be prefiltered by listing-relevant titles such as Will List, HODLer Airdrops, Launchpool, Megadrop, listing, or other official launch/listing language, but final qualification comes from the article detail body.

This is a source-completeness amendment only. It does not alter the DUAL definition, controls, horizons, gates, 2025 development period or 2026 holdout lock.

No Spot outcome had been opened before this amendment.
