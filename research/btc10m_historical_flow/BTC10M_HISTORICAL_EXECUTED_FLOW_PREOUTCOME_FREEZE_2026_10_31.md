## Time and observation causality
For every exact UTC 10-minute boundary t at minute %10 ==0 within each month, require 3 **fully closed one-minute** spot+perp candles at times (t-3m), (t-2m), (t-1m). Predict from t to t+600s using spot OPEN price at t (entry reference) and spot OPEN at t+10m (resolution reference), both on Binance SPOT, avoiding forward OHLC fields at decision. The signal calculation uses ONLY the historic previous three CLOSED 1m candles, source taker-buy quote amounts and quote volume; never the 10-minute future or current candle. Approximate synthetic as-of at t; tradability at exact opening price is unrealistic and NOT certified. Skip last 10-minute boundary per fixed month lacking in-month label; do not import next excluded month. Each 10-minute interval outcome is non-overlapping. If price(t+600s)>price(t), label UP; if lower DOWN; if EXACT equal TIE (settled as a zero payoff sensitivity only; do not claim MEXC exact tie rules). ## FROZEN two fixed signed trade-flow hypotheses and no future threshold search
For each venue v, trade taker buy share bv = SUM(taker_buy_quote over 3 closed minutes)/SUM(total_quote over 3 closed minutes); signed pressure = 2*bv-1, range [-1,1]. Total quote amounts finite positive and 0<=tak_buy<=total.
H1 PRIMARY FLOW-55: predict UP iff spot share >=0.55 AND perp share <=0.45; predict DOWN iff spot<=0.45 AND perp>=0.55, else ABSTAIN.
H2 STRICT FLOW-60: predict UP iff spot>=0.60 AND perp<=0.40; DOWN iff spot<=0.40 AND perp>=0.60; otherwise ABSTAIN. No persistence requirement (3-minute aggregated buy share, *not* 3 consecutive L2 snapshots). H2 is NOT an after-the-fact rescue: permanently fixed today before source. DO NOT select whichever looks better on OOS. Compare both with Bonferroni-adjusted one-sided confidence threshold 97.5%, as two tests. Promotion default always H1 unless H1 fails; H2 may qualify ONLY as new prospective research, not a backfit fix to the frozen future OKX/MEXC source.
Null/control: always UP in every exact 10-minute slot and spot-only momentum sign of last 3 fully CLOSED minutes (spot price from t-3m open to t open; the t open used only as the actual decision price, not t interval close), but display descriptively without promotion or adaptive thresholds. DO NOT call signal 50/50 without comparing day-side prevalence. Control baseline for sign stratification uses buy-share-only direction with bspot threshold 0.55 independent of futures as descriptive. No structural resampling or selection based on outcome.
