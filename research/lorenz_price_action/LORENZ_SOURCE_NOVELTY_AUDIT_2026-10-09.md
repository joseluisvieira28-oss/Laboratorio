## Source-described candidate mechanisms (paraphrased)
### LOR-RF (Realização Frustrada) — priority for deterministic feasibility
* In an uptrend: three successive candle directions down / up / down; in a downtrend: up / down / up.
* EMA9 and SMA21 must support prevailing direction; SMA200 must not obstruct intended target.
* Long trigger: breach the highest high of three signal candles; stop at the lowest low; target +1 initial risk unit (1R).
* Short: breach the lowest low of three; stop at highest high; target 1R.
* This is a CONTINUATION signal after corrective price action, NOT an independently proved stop hunt, nor necessarily a failed breakout.

### LOR-RLE (Rompimento de Lateralização Estreita)
* At least eight low-amplitude consolidation candles.
* A closed force/ignition candle breaks the range; EMA9 and SMA21 aligned; SMA200 must not obstruct target.
* Long/short entry via subsequent breach of force-candle extreme; protective stop at opposite extreme; target 2R.
* Source references an ignition candle wider than the last 20 and about 80% body in a related setup. It is NOT yet established that all these ignition conditions are mandatory for every RLE.
* Terms `low amplitude`, `force`, `trend`, `obstacle` are insufficiently numeric. A translation must be explicitly labelled lab-defined (not claimed as exact Lorenz) before outcomes.

### LOR-PR (Preço de Reversão) — secondary
* Bullish signal: candle makes a lower low than previous two candles and closes above previous close.
* Bearish: makes a higher high than previous two and closes below previous close.
* Favoured same-trend direction with EMA9/SMA21 and SMA200 obstacle filter.
* Entry by signal bar high/low, stop opposite signal extreme, target 1.5R.

## Novelty judgement
LOR-RF: PARTIAL_OVERLAP / DISTINCT_TESTABLE_CANDLE_SEQUENCE_CANDIDATE. Source supplies almost all signal geometry; still requires deterministic ex-ante definitions of trend/MA200 obstruction, trigger timing, gap handling and exits.
LOR-RLE: HIGH_OVERLAP_WITH_RANGE_BREAKOUT; SOURCE_RULES_UNDERDEFINED. Hold at RESEARCH_CANDIDATE until strict non-duplication map and deterministic range/ignition specification exist.
LOR-PR: PARTIAL_OVERLAP_WITH_GENERIC_REVERSAL_PATTERNS; LOWER_PRIORITY.
The trapped trader / forced-flow narrative is HYPOTHESIS only. Candles cannot prove positions or liquidation/stop-order flow.

## Cost and execution reality
Raw expectancy or a pattern win rate is not a tradable edge. Backtest must specify causally orderable stop entries, bid/ask, gap risk, intra-bar path, adverse fills, slippage, exit priority, venue-specific fees, funding and capacity. A gross 1R result is NOT net edge.
Prior MEXC cost examples are source/route-specific; no automatic assumption that a Binance Spot study earns MEXC Futures fills. Under no circumstances alter prior negative cost verdicts to rescue this family.
