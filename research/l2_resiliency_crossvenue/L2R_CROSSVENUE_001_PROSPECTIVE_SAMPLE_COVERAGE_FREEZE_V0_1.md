Full calendar 2024 is the Discovery universe, with all 366 official public spot BTCUSDT daily aggTrades archives verified against their official SHA256 checksums. A missing day, invalid row, timestamp/aggregate-ID order violation or checksum mismatch blocks the scientific result.

For each cell, selected parent R/Y joint availability must cover at least 99% of all positive-pre-depth sweep anchors. A paired day/cell requires at least 100 WEAK and 100 STRONG observations after independent timing gates. A global day requires all six cells; at least 300 global paired days are required. Every qualifying day is included; no calendar or best-cell selection is allowed. These round quality requirements demand broad calendar coverage and stable representation of both groups. They are governance minima, not a power analysis or an outcome-optimized choice.

External R baseline targets the actual selected parent R envelope timestamp, when the RR label becomes observable. External Y targets the actual selected parent Y envelope timestamp, preserving the parent's canonical sampled-state endpoints. Same-timestamp ties use the first aggregate trade ID in archive order. Parent Y timestamps are source-timing metadata, not parent price responses. The external baseline must strictly precede the endpoint.
