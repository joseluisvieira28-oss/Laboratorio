The frozen rule is implemented with a first-leg limit equal to the observed all-in price plus the scenario's slippage buffer. A partially filled hedge cancels the remainder and it settles unmatched. Snapshots are required at every 250 ms in the pre-decision 10-second window. The processor uses published settlement labels only in the PnL accounting loop and queues payment for use after the assumed recycle delay. Quoted size haircuts and slippage are model assumptions and not bounds on execution. Taker snapshot simulation cannot recover quote cancellations between captures or authenticate source event time. Minimum exchange notional and one-share precision are not modeled, so small-capital results are illustrative and cannot earn a live GO.
