# COINDESK20-REBALANCE-FORCED-FLOW-001 — LINKED PRODUCT TIMELINE SOURCE NOTE
Date: 2026-10-05
Status: SOURCE-ONLY CONTEXT; NO MARKET OUTCOMES

## Purpose
Establish whether CoinDesk 20 was connected to investable/tradable products during the development period, without using price outcomes.

## Public timeline
- 2024-01: CoinDesk 20 launched. Bullish offered CoinDesk 20 perpetual futures around launch.
- 2024-02-29: CoinDesk Indices announced Onramp Invest as the first US firm to deliver investable access to CoinDesk 20.
- 2024-09-16: CoinDesk Indices announced collaboration with 3iQ to launch funds globally that track CoinDesk 20.
- 2024-09-17: CoinDesk Indices announced BIT Exchange partnership for CoinDesk 20 perpetual contracts.
- By the 2025 CoinDesk 20 Q&A, linked-product partners included funds, trackers, ETPs, perpetuals, CFDs, options, structured products and strategies.
- The current CoinDesk 20 page reports >$16B total linked-product volume as of 2026-01-31; this later figure is context only and MUST NOT be back-projected as 2024-2025 AUM.

## Scientific caveat
This timeline proves a real linked-product ecosystem existed during the development window.

It does NOT prove:
- exact assets under management at each quarterly reconstitution;
- exact tracker replication method;
- exact forced notional per constituent;
- that every product physically bought/sold constituents at the implementation print.

Event-specific forced notional remains unknown and must be treated as a limitation rather than inferred from later product statistics.

No V0.2 gate or outcome rule is changed by this note.
