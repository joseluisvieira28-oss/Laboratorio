# CRYPTO LAB V2 — ADOPTION MATRIX V0.1
Date: 2026-10-07

## Rule
V2 must improve future decisions without contaminating experiments already in progress.

### A. New family, no outcomes opened
Adopt V2 immediately:
MECHANISM → ACCESSIBILITY → SOURCE → POWER → FREEZE → outcomes.

### B. Source-only family, no economic outcomes opened
May adopt V2 before activation.
Existing source evidence may be reused as source evidence.
No power or outcome conclusion may be inferred from source counts alone.

### C. Development/holdout already opened
Do NOT retrofit thresholds, H, alpha, direction or sample rules onto that same opened dataset.
Keep the old verdict immutable.
Use V2 only for a genuinely new untouched holdout/forward stage.

### D. Prospective/forward family already running under a valid freeze
Do NOT rewrite it mid-run.
V2 enters at the next separately frozen stage or at a new family fork with a new future boundary.

### E. Closed legacy family
Apply V2 only as cemetery triage.
Old outcomes can classify evidence quality and plan power.
They cannot create SURVIVES.
Any revival requires new/untouched data.

## Explicit anti-contamination rule
A V2 governance improvement is NOT authority to:
- recalculate old H after seeing old results;
- lower costs on the same old outcome and declare resurrection;
- change a failed horizon;
- reopen a closed holdout;
- replace a source because the original result looked weak;
- rerun until significance.
