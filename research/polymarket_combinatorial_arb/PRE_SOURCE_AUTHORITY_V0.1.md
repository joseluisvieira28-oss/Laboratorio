## Research question
Can mutually exclusive/exhaustive Polymarket outcome structures produce reproducible executable package mispricing after synchronous bid/ask, available size, fees and atomicity constraints?

This is not a claim that an edge exists.

## Primary family
RV / MARKET-STRUCTURE

## Mechanism
Where a set of outcome tokens is contractually exhaustive and mutually exclusive, the terminal payoff of the complete package is mechanically bounded by the settlement rules. Fragmented liquidity and independent order books may temporarily price the package away from that bound. The potential payer is segmentation, inventory constraints, stale quotes, thin books, attention asymmetry and non-atomic execution risk.
