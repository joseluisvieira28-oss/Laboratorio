# LIQMAP-001B — Direct Hyperliquid position price exploratory one-day check
Pre-registered 2026-11-26, after free sample SOURCE coverage only, BEFORE seeing prediction outcomes.

This is a distinct study from the Bybit OI synthetic proxy (SOURCE_BLOCKED) and from Neuron. Data FREE public LiquidVision hf sample, hl_positions_sample.csv (76,224 records, 28 unique snapshots only on 2026-11-08, with BTC/ETH/SOL and exchange-reported liq_px), available via huggingface.co/datasets/liquidvision/crypto-derivatives-sample. NOT a market-wide full-liquidity map: provider samples only large Hyperliquid positions; repeated wallets, cross margin and changing positions make coverage incomplete.

At each snapshot t and coin, compute median mark_px of all positive finite position marks. Discard positions with invalid side, liq_px, notional; only side=long liquidation below mark and side=short liquidation above mark (diagnostic filter, not assumption of every account). Aggregate position value_usd into fixed 0.5% log-price bins around mark; only absolute log distance 0.005..0.05. Select strongest band by mass*exp(-distance/0.03) (thresholds frozen) and classify UP if band above current mark, DOWN if below; NO_SIGNAL if no band.

Take decisions from observation times spaced >=2h, after at least 1h of earlier history, and before >=1h future history. Label is direction of mark at nearest snapshot at or after t+60m and no later than t+120m, compared with mark_t; no intraperiod touching claims, no future info in selection. Baseline momentum of mark at t vs nearest snapshot at or before t−60m (not older than 120m). Only score matched eligible cases; outcomes up/down/flat, no prediction for flat. Count independent times (NOT rows), Wilson 95% per coin, leave out actual order sizing/profit/trading due sparse 1-day data. Data sources and SHA256 and actual timestamp stored.

STATISTICAL GATE: <20 non-overlapping times per coin => INCONCLUSIVE regardless apparent accuracy. Even >=20 from one day cannot prove edge due single regime / multiple correlated symbols / no real trade fees. Do not choose parameters or label by looking at outcomes. No Neuron claims. No money, accounts, private IDs or trades, no positions/account addresses in output.
