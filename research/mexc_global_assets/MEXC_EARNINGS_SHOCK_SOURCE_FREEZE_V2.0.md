# MEXC EARNINGS-SHOCK CATCH-UP — SOURCE GATE V2.0

Date: 2026-10-05
Status: SOURCE-ONLY / PRE-OUTCOME

## Untouched period
2026-07-01 through 2026-08-31.

The September/October outcome windows used by prior global-asset studies are excluded.

## Event authority
Public SEC submissions only.

An event is:
- Form 8-K;
- Item 2.02 (Results of Operations and Financial Condition);
- filing date in July/August 2026.

To avoid same-day timing ambiguity and look-ahead, the eligible trading session is conservatively fixed as the next weekday after the SEC filing date. Same-day premarket releases are therefore intentionally not used.

## Market source requirement
For an event to pass source coverage, MEXC target, Binance Futures external and Bitget Futures external must all provide public 1-minute transport over 12:20–13:40 UTC on the event session.

No price returns, gaps, signals, directions, wins/losses or PnL may be computed in this source gate.

No private endpoints, account reads, wallets, orders, exchange mutation or live trading.

Any outcome study requires a separate pre-outcome freeze after this gate.
