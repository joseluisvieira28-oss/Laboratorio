# CRYPTO LAB — CANDIDATE PROMOTION REGISTRY
Last updated: 2026-10-05

Purpose:
Maintain a conservative promotion ladder for economically interesting candidates.
A candidate may be impressive without being executable. Labels must reflect the strongest evidence actually earned.

## Promotion ladder
- STONE / CLOSED — failed the relevant scientific gate.
- SIGNAL / PHENOMENON — real descriptive effect, not yet a candidate for execution.
- QUASE DIAMANTE INFORMACIONAL — strong, repeatable information effect with meaningful robustness, but no validated executable edge.
- QUASE DIAMANTE EXECUTÁVEL — survives a legitimate modeled execution holdout but is not yet forward/live validated.
- DIAMANTE — reserved for a candidate that survives confirmatory execution evidence and independent forward validation under realistic costs and governance.
- LIVE-PROVEN — separate later status; not implied by DIAMANTE.
