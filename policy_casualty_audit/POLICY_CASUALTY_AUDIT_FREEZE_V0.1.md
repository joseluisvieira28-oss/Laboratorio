## Mission

Audit whether historically promising Crypto Lab candidates were killed, blocked, not promoted, or materially delayed mainly by governance / promotion-policy / statistical / sample / operational hurdles rather than by actual economic falsification.

This is a policy-process audit, not a rescue program.

## Authority precedence

1. Candidate-specific frozen authorities and protected-data contracts.
2. Governance V4 / Death Audit.
3. Promotion Policy V3.
4. Edge Classification Board.
5. Adversarial Review System V0.1.
6. This audit freeze.

Where stricter prior authority applies, it controls.

## Immutable rules

- No historical verdict is rewritten.
- No sealed/protected outcome is opened without existing authority.
- No new threshold, asset, direction, horizon, period, source, cost, or subgroup may be chosen from outcomes to rescue an exact candidate.
- BLOCKED / SOURCE_BLOCKED / DATA_FAILURE / PROVENANCE_FAILURE / TECHNICAL_FAILURE are not NO_EDGE unless primary authority explicitly establishes economic falsification.
- Positive diagnostics are not automatically trading edges.
- Forecast/mechanism evidence is separated from executable monetization evidence.
- Historical candidate and later standalone/new identity are never conflated.
