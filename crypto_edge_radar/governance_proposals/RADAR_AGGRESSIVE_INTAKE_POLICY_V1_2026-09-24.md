**Purpose:** add legitimate forward-observation motors to the Radar early, while preserving the distinction between observation maturity and economic trading maturity.

## 1. Core rule

A candidate does NOT need Tier 2 merely to become a Radar shadow/watch motor.

Radar intake is allowed when all of the following are true:
- immutable candidate identity exists;
- mechanism/rule is frozen prospectively;
- canonical source or forward source contract exists;
- watcher/collector can operate fail-closed;
- no retrospective backfill is used to manufacture forward evidence;
- current scientific state is represented truthfully;
- the motor cannot create capital or orders merely by being in Radar.

Radar intake is observability, not promotion.

## 2. Micro-live rule

The separate policy TIER2-MICROLIVE-POLICY-V1.0-FROZEN-2026-09-24 controls capital eligibility.

Once a Radar candidate legitimately reaches Tier 2:
- historical science is not repeated;
- later Tier1/Diamond-strength forward gates do not automatically become blanket micro-live blockers;
- candidate becomes eligible for candidate-specific MICRO-LIVE RESEARCH AUTHORITY review;
- exact source/signal/route/firewall gates still apply;
- an exact no-peek/protected lane retains precedence if live observation would contaminate it.

Tier 3 or lower never becomes micro-live merely because it is visible in Radar.

## 3. Radar motor classes

### RADAR_ACTIVE_SHADOW
Scientifically legitimate forward/shadow motor with current frozen source/watcher.

### RADAR_INTAKE_READY
All requirements for a new Radar motor are already satisfied; technical integration/deployment may proceed without further scientific waiting.

### RADAR_FISHING_QUEUE
Mechanism/source/forward work is legitimate, but a required source/calibration/economic/adjudication gate is still incomplete. It may be watched by external automation but must not be represented as an active promoted Radar motor.

### RADAR_EXCLUDED_TERMINAL
Exact mechanism is NO_EDGE / promotion-fail / terminally closed. Do not motorize or rescue.

## 4. No arbitrary waiting

Do not impose generic 30-day, 60-day, 90-day or N-trade waits unless they are part of an already-frozen scientific contract.

Do not create new waiting thresholds merely because a candidate has entered Radar.

When a required gate can be adjudicated from already-legitimate evidence, adjudicate it immediately.
