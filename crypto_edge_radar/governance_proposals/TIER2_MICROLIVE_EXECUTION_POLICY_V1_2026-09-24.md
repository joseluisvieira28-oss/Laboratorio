# CRYPTO LAB — TIER 2 MICRO-LIVE EXECUTION POLICY V1 — 2026-09-24

**Policy ID:** TIER2-MICROLIVE-POLICY-V1.0-FROZEN-2026-09-24  
**Status:** FROZEN / PROSPECTIVE / ADDITIVE  
**Purpose:** permit tightly bounded real-money micro-live research for scientifically validated Tier-2 candidates before Tier-1/Diamond completion, without changing frozen science.

## 1. Core policy change

Tier 2 is now sufficient scientific maturity to become eligible for a candidate-specific MICRO-LIVE RESEARCH AUTHORITY.

A candidate does NOT need to become Tier 1, Diamond, or finish every later prospective-strengthening gate before it may use tiny real-money research capital.

This policy does not make any candidate executable by itself. It changes the governance eligibility rule only.

## 2. What does NOT change

This policy may not:
- rewrite any historical verdict;
- change signals, assets, symbols, direction semantics, thresholds, horizons, costs or entry/exit rules;
- tune a candidate after outcomes;
- convert Tier 3 into Tier 2;
- resurrect Tier 4 / NO_EDGE;
- convert a source blocker into a scientific PASS;
- ignore stale source/account/market state;
- allow late-entry chasing;
- waive candidate identity or execution-route compatibility.

## 3. Forward-gate reclassification

For a Tier-2 candidate, an existing later forward/diamond gate is no longer automatically a blanket micro-live prohibition.

Unless an exact higher-precedence candidate authority explicitly requires the gate before ANY capital observation and has not been prospectively amended, such a gate is classified as:

TIER1_OR_DIAMOND_EVIDENCE_GATE

and not:

MICROLIVE_ELIGIBILITY_GATE.

Candidate-specific amendments may prospectively separate:
A) micro-live research eligibility; from
B) Tier-1/Diamond promotion evidence.

Historical gate results and thresholds remain untouched.

## 4. Micro-live is research, not promotion

A micro-live trade:
- gives zero automatic Tier promotion;
- gives zero automatic size increase;
- may be used as prospective execution evidence only under a frozen evidence contract;
- may not trigger parameter rescue after a loss;
- may not trigger scaling after a win.

## 5. Default hard micro-live envelope

Unless a stricter candidate-specific rule exists:

- maximum live notional per position: 10 USDT equivalent;
- leverage: exactly 1x;
- margin mode for futures: isolated;
- maximum concurrent live positions across this V1 program: 1;
- maximum account live notional exposure: 10 USDT equivalent;
- daily realized-loss kill: 2 USDT;
- rolling 7-day realized-loss kill: 5 USDT;
- averaging down: forbidden;
- martingale: forbidden;
- revenge sizing: forbidden;
- automatic size escalation: forbidden;
- cross margin: forbidden;
- Auto Margin Add: OFF;
- order must satisfy current venue minimum without exceeding the 10 USDT cap;
- if venue minimum > 10 USDT: NO_TRADE;
- exact current fee/funding/slippage feasibility must pass;
- emergency global kill switch required.

These are execution-governance limits, not scientific parameters.

## 7. Automatic execution boundary

Tier-2 scientific eligibility is necessary but not sufficient for automated execution.

Automatic micro-live additionally requires:
- candidate-specific frozen execution amendment;
- candidate explicitly allowlisted;
- exact symbol/direction/venue identity;
- healthy/fresh canonical source;
- canonical signal within TTL;
- fresh account state;
- fresh market state;
- hard firewall PASS;
- exactly-once intent;
- no conflicting position/order;
- current venue minimum within frozen notional cap;
- immutable pre-order receipt;
- tested restart/reconciliation path.

Any unknown, stale, mismatch or duplicate state = NO_ORDER.
