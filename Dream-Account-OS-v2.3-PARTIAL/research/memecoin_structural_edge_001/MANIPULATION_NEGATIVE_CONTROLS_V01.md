# MSEL-001 — MANIPULATION TAXONOMY & NEGATIVE CONTROLS V0.1

Status: RESEARCH-ONLY / FAIL-CLOSED
Branch: `memecoin-structural-edge-v0.1`
Date: 2026-09-15

## 1. Purpose

Translate recent large-scale memecoin manipulation research into falsifiable MSEL-001 controls without assuming that manipulation detection automatically creates tradable alpha.

Recent work on pump.fun identifies five recurring manipulation classes at scale:
1. wash trading;
2. creator-address obfuscation;
3. coordinated selling;
4. copycat coins;
5. social-media manipulation.

These are treated as risk mechanisms and candidate confounders, not as proven trading signals.

## 2. Wash / synthetic-activity risk

On a bonding curve, gross volume can be inflated without broadening the economic buyer base.

Candidate point-in-time diagnostics at T+5m:
- gross turnover vs net SOL inflow;
- same-wallet buy/sell round trips;
- fraction of volume generated by top-1/top-3 wallets;
- repeat-trade share;
- unique external buyers / transaction count;
- median external-wallet net position change;
- burst concentration by slot/time bucket;
- transaction-size repetition / low-entropy sizing;
- wallet-cluster-adjusted unique buyer count.

### New candidate feature: Organicity Ratio

`ORGANICITY = independent_external_net_inflow / gross_turnover`

where protocol addresses, creator-linked wallets, known Mayhem activity and identified same-funder clusters are excluded from the numerator.

Hypothesis: a launch with large gross volume but weak independent net inflow is more fragile than a launch with comparable curve progress driven by genuinely distinct net buyers.

This feature is NOT promoted until it beats price/volume-only baselines OOS.

## 3. Creator-address obfuscation

A visible creator address is not assumed to identify the full economic actor.

Pre-decision graph features may include only history observable before the current launch:
- shared funder among creator / early buyers;
- prior transfers between creator and early buyers;
- same-payer patterns;
- repeated co-occurrence across earlier launches;
- prior launch factory count;
- time since funding / wallet age;
- creator-linked cluster size based only on prior graph edges.

Future co-occurrence is forbidden.

### Negative control

Compare creator-cluster features with activity-matched placebo wallets. If the apparent signal is explained by generic wallet activity rather than creator linkage, the feature fails.

## 4. Coordinated selling

Future coordinated dumps cannot be used as pre-entry features. Only coordination already observable by T+5m is eligible.

Possible early diagnostics:
- synchronous first sells by multiple early buyers before decision time;
- seller overlap with prior launch cohorts known before launch;
- common-funder early sellers;
- abnormal similarity in trade timing/size among a prior-defined wallet cohort.

The later dump itself is an outcome/risk event, never a feature.

## 5. Copycat risk — NEW independent attack

Copycat launches are structurally different from original narratives and may be cheaply mass-produced.

Point-in-time candidate features using only launches already seen before the current token:
- normalized name similarity to recent launches;
- exact / near-exact symbol reuse;
- metadata URI reuse;
- image/content hash reuse where metadata is retrievable at launch time;
- website/domain reuse;
- social-handle reuse;
- minutes since the earliest matching narrative/token;
- creator cluster's prior copycat frequency.

### Hard rule

Similarity corpus must be strictly historical. A token cannot be labelled a copycat using a later-famous token that did not yet exist.

## 6. Social manipulation

Current follower counts or present-day social pages are NOT valid historical features.

Social features remain QUARANTINED unless an auditable point-in-time source can reconstruct what was visible at decision time.

Permitted before such a source exists:
- presence/absence of social links embedded in launch metadata as-of creation;
- exact domain/handle reuse against prior launches;
- metadata completeness fields stored on-chain or content-addressed at launch.

Forbidden without archival evidence:
- later follower counts;
- later engagement;
- later account verification;
- manually reconstructed hype scores using hindsight.

## 7. Mandatory negative controls

Every candidate structural feature family must survive the relevant placebo tests:

1. **Price/volume baseline** — does structure add information beyond early price/volume/curve progress?
2. **Activity-matched wallet placebo** — protects against the RED-COHORT lesson that highly active wallets self-select into high-flow launches.
3. **Random wallet-label permutation** — checks whether identity relationships actually matter.
4. **Creator-link placebo** — compare true prior links to matched unrelated high-activity wallets.
5. **Regime stratification** — Mayhem, quote asset, fee schedule and creation variants remain separated.
6. **Time-shift sanity test** — any feature accidentally improved by future graph construction is leakage and invalidates the run.
7. **Duplicate-launch / factory control** — prevent a prolific actor from leaking nearly identical examples across train/test.
8. **Day/week cluster robustness** — signal cannot depend on one viral narrative day.

## 8. Grouped temporal splitting

Chronological split alone is insufficient if the same creator/factory emits many near-identical launches around the boundary.

Primary validation therefore requires:
- chronological train/dev/test;
- creator/factory-group awareness;
- sensitivity test excluding creator clusters seen in training from the strongest OOS claim.

If performance collapses on unseen creator clusters, classify the result as actor-reputation memorization rather than general structural edge.

## 9. Ranked hypotheses after this attack

### H1 — Economic concentration > visible holder concentration
Early wallets that appear independent but share funding/history create hidden concentration and elevated downside risk.

Priority: VERY HIGH.

### H2 — Organicity Ratio > raw volume
Independent net demand should be more informative than gross turnover that can be manufactured.

Priority: VERY HIGH.

### H3 — Creator/factory history contains PIT risk information
Repeated launch behavior may create a persistent actor-level risk signal.

Priority: HIGH, but must survive unseen-creator validation.

### H4 — Copycat/reuse structure predicts fragility
Metadata/narrative reuse may identify industrial low-quality launch factories before price failure.

Priority: HIGH and relatively cheap to test.

### H5 — Persistent sniper cohorts predict winners
Current external evidence does NOT justify this as a standalone causal hypothesis; activity-matched placebo effects are too strong.

Priority: LOW unless combined with launch-quality controls.

## 10. Stop rule

No manipulation-derived feature is promoted because it is intuitive or because external papers classify fraud well.

It must improve temporal OOS economic outcomes, retain a meaningful share of future winners, survive placebos, and remain useful after realistic costs.

No live trading. No post-outcome rescue. No merge to main.
