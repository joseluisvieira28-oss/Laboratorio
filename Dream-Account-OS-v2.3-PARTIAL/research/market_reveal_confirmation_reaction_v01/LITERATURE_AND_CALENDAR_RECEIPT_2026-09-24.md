# MRCR V0.1 — Literature + Calendar Receipt
As of: 2026-09-24

## Independent mechanism evidence

1. Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov, *The Price Impact of Order Book Events*, Journal of Financial Econometrics.
Short-horizon price changes are strongly related to order-flow imbalance and the impact slope varies inversely with market depth.
Relevance: motivates joint measurement of flow, price response and depth rather than raw flow alone.

2. *The probability of informed trading measured with price impact, price reversal, and volatility* (2016).
Informed-flow states are associated with meaningful price impact and comparatively little subsequent reversal.
Relevance: supports the conceptual distinction between persistent price discovery and temporary impact.

3. Anastasopoulos et al., *Order flow and cryptocurrency returns*, Journal of Financial Markets 79 (2026), 101047.
Reports predictive information in international order flow for crypto returns and a permanent component of price impact.
Relevance: mechanism support only; its horizon differs from MRCR.

4. Kroner, Mohammed & Vega, Federal Reserve Board, *How Do Cryptocurrencies Price Economic News?* (2026).
Reports sharp macro-announcement increases in crypto volatility, trading volume and bid-ask spreads, elevated for roughly 30 minutes, with order flow playing a role in price discovery.
Relevance: supports the macro-event state as a high-information environment, not a guaranteed continuation rule.

5. Schmalz, *Order Flow Imbalance and Short-Horizon BTC/USDT Returns: A Signal That Kept Needing More Scrutiny* (2026 working paper).
Reports materially changing out-of-sample behavior as the capture length expanded.
Relevance: caution against short samples, single-split confidence and threshold mining.
