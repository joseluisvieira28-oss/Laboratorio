## Search conclusion

The census checked Reuters discovery, public institutional research, historical calendar
families, BLS archives, FRED/ALFRED, and public archives. Accessible Reuters and other news
hits were commonly published after T0. Current calendar pages lacked an immutable historical
snapshot or source chain. Search-engine snippets were never accepted as evidence. Details are
in `receipts/2021_SEARCH_RECEIPT.csv` and `2021_SOURCE_YIELD.csv`.

“Not found” is the defensible conclusion for the free/public search performed here. It is not
a claim that no qualifying evidence ever existed. Recovering it probably requires an
institutional news archive with historical publication metadata or licensed poll data.
