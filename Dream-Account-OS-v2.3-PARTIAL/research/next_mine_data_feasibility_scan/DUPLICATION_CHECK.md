# Next Mine — Duplication Check

Date: 2026-09-21

## Method

Branches were compared by economic mechanism, information set, predictor clock, source,
universe, horizon and decision state. A different branch, asset, threshold or timeframe does
not create a distinct hypothesis.

## Anti-rebranding rule

Candidates classified `EXACT_DUPLICATE`, `NEAR_DUPLICATE` or
`ECONOMICALLY_EQUIVALENT` are `DUPLICATE` in the feasibility matrix and cannot be selected.
A future proposal must introduce a materially different causal state and information source,
not merely a parameter, timeframe, venue or asset substitution.
