# CRYPTO LAB — PROMOTION POLICY V3 — AGGRESSIVE

**Policy ID:** ND-PROMOTION-POLICY-V3.0-FROZEN  
**Status:** FROZEN / PROSPECTIVE GLOBAL POLICY  
**Freeze date:** 2026-09-17  
**Scope:** all current and future Crypto Lab candidates. Historical V1/V2 verdicts remain immutable.

## 1. Principle

AGGRESSIVE does not mean lower standards after outcomes. It means extracting the maximum legitimate information from evidence that already exists, under rules frozen before V3 adjudication, and removing arbitrary waiting requirements that add little information.

## 2. States outside the tier ladder

DATA_FAILURE, TECHNICAL_FAILURE, BLOCKED, PROVENANCE_FAILURE, SOURCE_INADEQUATE, INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE, NOT_TESTED and replication inconclusive solely because required source authority is unavailable remain non-scientific unresolved states. They are not Tier 4 and must not be relabelled NO_EDGE.

## 3. Universal hard eligibility

Tier 1 or Tier 2 requires all of:
- clean provenance, reproducible authority and immutable candidate identity;
- no leakage, hindsight, post-outcome sign change, asset/subperiod selection or parameter rescue;
- realistic frozen base costs and execution assumptions;
- positive base-net expectancy and Profit Factor > 1 for evidence used to support promotion;
- expected relationship/sign preserved;
- execution feasible in the intended market;
- statistical uncertainty and concentration/tail risk reported;
- no adequate independent OOS/holdout block that materially destroys base-cost economics for the exact candidate.

Stress-cost failure alone is a fragility flag when base costs are demonstrably realistic; it is not an automatic Tier 2 veto. Lowering costs after outcomes is forbidden.

## 4. Sample adequacy

Sample adequacy is mechanism-aware and must be frozen before new outcomes.

### Dense/systematic mechanisms
Tier 2 normally requires at least 100 resolved observations in the canonical corpus and at least one genuinely independent validation block with at least 50 observations, unless an earlier prospectively frozen mechanism-specific minimum is stricter.

### Rare/event-driven mechanisms — Rare-Event Replicated Corpus Path
Tier 2 may use this path when all of:
- at least 30 resolved events across at least 3 disjoint historical blocks;
- at least 2 blocks genuinely independent from original Discovery selection;
- every supporting block has positive base-net expectancy and PF > 1 under unchanged rules/costs;
- pooled base-net expectancy > 0 and pooled PF > 1;
- every leave-one-block-out pooled result remains base-net positive with PF > 1;
- pooled largest known single positive event contribution <= 40% of pooled positive PnL;
- no event deletion, event filter, horizon, pair, direction, threshold or cost change after outcomes.

A small independent block may have high within-block concentration; under V3 that is a mandatory fragility flag, not an automatic veto, when the replicated corpus tests above pass.

## 5. Independent replication

Independence must be real: untouched OOS/holdout, pre-Discovery historical block, or genuinely prospective block defined without using its outcomes. Splitting one optimized sample does not create independence. Reusing the same outcomes under a cosmetic label does not create independence.

## 6. Temporal stability, concentration and tails

For dense/systematic mechanisms, Tier 2 requires at least half of major predeclared temporal blocks positive and no adequate independent validation block materially negative after base costs.

For rare/event-driven mechanisms, replicated-block consistency and leave-one-block-out robustness may substitute for a fixed year-count rule. Negative subperiods remain disclosed as fragility.

A maximum drawdown above 50% is a HIGH-RISK fragility flag and blocks micro-live until a separate prospectively frozen risk envelope is approved, but does not by itself erase replicated positive edge evidence at Tier 2. Tier 1 requires an acceptable risk envelope for intended deployment.

## 7. Tier definitions

### Tier 1 — Validated Edge
Meets Tier 2; has strong independent confirmation beyond Discovery; uncertainty, temporal stability, tail dependence and execution are acceptable; at least one prospectively frozen shadow/forward validation has demonstrated operational integrity; and no unresolved material contradiction remains. Tier 1 alone does not authorize production capital.

### Tier 2 — Promoted Candidate / Quase Diamante
Meets universal hard eligibility and either:
- **Standard Replication Path:** statistically supportive pre-specified evidence (normally one-sided/equivalent p <= 0.10) plus positive base economics and temporal support, **or** a genuinely independent OOS/holdout that is base-net positive/PF>1 with adequate sample and no catastrophic concentration; or
- **Rare-Event Replicated Corpus Path** above.

Tier 2 is permission to accelerate validation, not permission to assume the edge is permanent.

### Tier 3 — Watchlist
Usable positive or mixed evidence that fails one or more Tier 2 requirements but is not scientifically rejected. Includes strong Discovery not sufficiently replicated, positive economics with unacceptable unresolved concentration/temporal fragility, or candidates awaiting a narrowly defined prospective test.

### Tier 4 — Rejected
Adequate independent evidence materially contradicts the exact candidate: materially negative base-net expectancy, PF materially below 1, wrong sign, or independent validation destroys the effect. Historical positive evidence remains recorded. A Tier 4 exact candidate cannot be resurrected by V3 without materially new independent evidence under a new prospective authority.

## 8. Historical re-adjudication rule

V1 and V2 verdicts are immutable. V3 may only add:

**HISTORICAL VERDICT PRESERVED / RE-ADJUDICATED UNDER PROMOTION POLICY V3.**

Already-opened immutable ledgers may be recombined only through diagnostics explicitly specified by V3 before V3 adjudication and only when signal, costs, direction, horizon, event population and trade inclusion are unchanged. Such diagnostics may assess pooled robustness, leave-one-block-out stability and concentration; they may not manufacture a new strategy.

## 9. Shadow and micro-live research

Tier 2 must have a frozen shadow specification and execution preflight. For dense strategies, shadow duration/sample is set prospectively from expected signal frequency.

For rare event-driven Tier 2 candidates that qualified through the replicated-corpus path, there is no mandatory wait for an arbitrary number such as 25 new events before micro-live research. After operational shadow/preflight passes, a separately frozen MICRO-LIVE RESEARCH AUTHORITY may permit the first genuinely prospective event to be observed/executed with a tiny fixed research risk budget, no post-outcome tuning, explicit kill-switches and full reconciliation.

Micro-live is data collection, not proof of Tier 1 and not production authorization.

## 10. Tier 1 and production ladder

Tier 2 -> frozen shadow/preflight -> separately authorized micro-live research -> Tier 1 adjudication after prospectively defined evidence -> production consideration under a separate capital/risk authority.

Production requires a new authorization; no tier automatically creates orders, exchange mutation, leverage, alerts/webhooks or capital deployment.

## 11. Governance

Research-first; fail-closed; no cherry-picking; no post-outcome tuning; no historical verdict rewriting; no deleted negative evidence; no fake independence; no fabricated samples; no cost reduction rescue; no direction changes after outcomes; no favorable subperiod selection; no main merge without separate authorization.

Previously protected 2026/live data remain protected unless a specific V3 authority explicitly opens them before access.
