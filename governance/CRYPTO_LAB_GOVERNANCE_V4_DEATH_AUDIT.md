## 1. Audit result

The Crypto Lab does not have one kind of "dead lab". It has at least four materially different states:

1. **Scientifically terminal exact experiments** — valid frozen evidence rejected the exact candidate. No rescue.
2. **Operationally terminal but scientifically unresolved experiments** — source, data, provenance, technical or sample blockers closed the exact run without an economic verdict.
3. **Archived/superseded references** — storage/engineering state only; archive status does not determine science.
4. **Active/protected lineages** — still collecting source, replication, shadow or prospective evidence under existing authority.

Governance V4 formalizes those distinctions and blocks resurrection by stale indexes or branch names.

## 12. Final verdict

The lab should stop treating "dead branch", "blocked lab" and "dead mechanism" as synonyms.

The scientifically defensible operating model is:

`EXACT RESULT IMMUTABLE -> LIFECYCLE CLASSIFIED -> BLOCKER TRIGGERED OR TERMINAL -> NEW MECHANISM ONLY THROUGH NEW-ID GATE`

This preserves negative evidence, prevents zombie source hunts, prevents resurrection, and still allows genuinely new mechanisms to survive without contaminating them with hindsight.
