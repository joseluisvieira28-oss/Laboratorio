# NEAR-DIAMONDS PROMOTION POLICY V4.0 — AGGRESSIVE EVIDENCE LADDER
Date: 2026-10-07
Status: FROZEN PROSPECTIVELY

## Purpose

V4 separates scientific truth from promotion conservatism.

Scientific verdicts remain immutable. A candidate does not need final Tier-1 confirmation before it can be promoted to an EXPERIMENTAL_READY state for operational preparation.

Core rule:
- science decides whether the mechanism survives;
- governance decides how quickly a surviving mechanism advances through readiness stages.

Large-sample gates control validation and scale-readiness, not whether a mature candidate may begin operational-readiness work.

## Non-negotiable scientific firewall

V4 MUST NOT:
- relabel NO_EDGE / FAIL / SOURCE_BLOCKED / INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE as PASS;
- change any frozen signal, direction, horizon, control, threshold, source, cost assumption or inclusion rule after outcomes;
- reopen quarantined outcomes;
- use later evidence to rescue a failed earlier gate;
- delete losing observations;
- reduce assumed costs because a result is unattractive;
- count operational observations as independent scientific confirmation unless a separate pre-outcome protocol explicitly allowed that role.

## Evidence ladder

### L0 — RESEARCH
Source gate, discovery, calibration or forward collection only.

### L1 — EXPERIMENTAL_READY
Operational preparation is permitted when ALL hold:
1. valid positive scientific state exists: Discovery PASS, OOS PASS, Tier-2 promotion, replicated mechanism evidence or equivalent;
2. no terminal scientific failure applies to the exact frozen rule;
3. exact executable identity is already frozen or can be frozen prospectively without opening protected outcomes;
4. source and timestamp integrity are clean;
5. no unresolved fatal duplicate, identity, venue or reconciliation defect exists;
6. candidate-specific readiness receipt is committed before any execution authority.

L1 does NOT itself authorize trading, orders, wallets, exchange mutation or spending.

### L2 — MICRO_LIVE_READY
A candidate may be promoted from L1 to MICRO_LIVE_READY after either:
- at least 10 clean prospective operational observations; OR
- a rare-event exception; OR
- a dense-systematic exception.

MICRO_LIVE_READY remains a readiness classification only. Any real execution requires a separate explicit authority outside this policy.

### L3 — VALIDATED
Requires the candidate's existing scientific forward/OOS/confirmation standard to pass.
V4 does not lower those scientific gates.

### L4 — PRODUCTION_READY
Outside this policy.
Requires separate production, portfolio-risk and operational authority.

## Rare-event exception

A rare-event candidate may reach MICRO_LIVE_READY without an arbitrary 10/25/50 new-event requirement when:
- >=30 historical events exist across >=3 disjoint evidence blocks;
- at least two blocks are independent from original Discovery selection;
- every supporting block is positive under the immutable rule;
- pooled PF >1;
- every leave-one-block-out pooled result remains positive and PF>1;
- pooled largest known positive-event concentration <=40%;
- provenance is clean.

This affects readiness only. It does not manufacture Tier 1 or scientific confirmation.

## Dense/systematic exception

A dense candidate with prior OOS/Tier-2 scientific PASS may reach MICRO_LIVE_READY before its large forward validation gate completes if:
- >=10 genuinely prospective forward resolutions exist OR historical OOS contains >=100 clean resolved observations;
- authoritative base-cost economics are positive;
- source/collector identity is clean;
- all known fragility flags remain explicit.

Forward N=50/100 requirements remain validation gates, not readiness gates.

## Sealed-outcome exception

V4 cannot override a pre-existing no-early-outcome firewall.

If operational execution or inspection would necessarily reveal or contaminate a sealed scientific outcome, the candidate remains READINESS_ONLY until that boundary expires.

## Candidate-specific readiness receipt

Every L1/L2 candidate must freeze:
- immutable scientific identity;
- exact instrument/venue mapping;
- entry/exit semantics;
- timing requirements;
- source and duplicate controls;
- known fragility;
- failure/abort behavior;
- reconciliation requirements;
- whether operational evidence may count scientifically (default: NO).

## V4 philosophy

V4 is aggressive in PROMOTION SPEED and strict in SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY.

The default response to a scientifically mature candidate becomes:
PROMOTE TO READINESS NOW; USE FUTURE EVIDENCE FOR VALIDATION/SCALE.

It is no longer:
WAIT FOR TIER 1 BEFORE DOING ANY OPERATIONAL PREPARATION.

## Governance

- no main merge;
- no live trading or orders from this policy;
- no wallets, exchange mutation or spending;
- no authenticated/private endpoint unless a later explicit authority permits a read-only preflight;
- fail closed on uncertainty;
- preserve receipts;
- no post-outcome scientific tuning.
