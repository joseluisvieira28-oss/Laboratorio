# CRYPTO LAB — GOVERNANCE V4 / DEATH POLICY

Policy ID: `CRYPTO-LAB-GOVERNANCE-V4.0-DEATH-FROZEN`  
Status: **FROZEN / ADDITIVE / NON-RETROACTIVE**  
Scope: exploration, continuation, closure, dormancy, supersession and archival governance.  
Promotion tiers remain governed by the already-frozen Promotion Policy V3 unless a later prospectively frozen promotion policy is separately authorized.

## 1. Non-negotiable preservation rule

Governance V4 does not rewrite science.

All previously frozen hypotheses, protocols, thresholds, sources, costs, samples, protected periods, closeouts, positive evidence, negative evidence, failures and historical classifications remain immutable.

V4 may classify the lifecycle of an experiment or mechanism going forward. It may not:
- change an old scientific verdict;
- reinterpret a failed gate as a pass;
- relabel BLOCKED / DATA_FAILURE / PROVENANCE_FAILURE / INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE as NO_EDGE;
- relabel NO_EDGE / TIER 4 / rejected exact candidates as unresolved;
- delete negative evidence;
- manufacture independence;
- open protected outcomes to decide whether a new design is attractive;
- rescue by changing asset, timeframe, direction, threshold, horizon, cost, subperiod, source clock or sample after outcomes.

## 2. Authority precedence

When repository or Drive artifacts disagree, use the following precedence for the exact experiment identity:

1. Frozen experiment protocol plus its canonical execution receipt and terminal closeout.
2. Candidate-specific prospectively frozen amendment that was authorized before the affected outcomes were opened.
3. Frozen global policy applicable at the time of adjudication.
4. Current canonical classification board / research registry.
5. Archive plan, branch census, roadmap or feasibility scan.
6. Branch name, commit message, chat shorthand or folder location.

A later index may summarize an earlier result but cannot alter it. An older index can never reopen a later closeout.

Operational metadata and scientific authority are separate. A branch being archived, active, renamed or superseded does not by itself determine scientific truth.

## 3. Three independent state axes

Every lab must be representable on three separate axes.

### A. Scientific verdict
Examples: `NOT_TESTED`, `SOURCE_BLOCKED`, `DATA_FAILURE`, `PROVENANCE_FAILURE`, `TECHNICAL_FAILURE`, `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`, `NO_EDGE`, `TIER_4_REJECTED`, `TIER_3`, `TIER_2`, `TIER_1`.

### B. Operational lifecycle
`ACTIVE`, `DORMANT_TRIGGERED`, `CLOSED_EXACT`, `SUPERSEDED_PRE_OUTCOME`, `ARCHIVED`.

### C. Mechanism state
`SUPPORTED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `FALSIFIED_EXACT`, `CONTINUATION_ELIGIBLE_NEW_ID`, `EXHAUSTED_WITHIN_DEFINED_SCOPE`.

These axes must never be collapsed into one label. In particular:
- `ARCHIVED` is not a scientific verdict.
- `CLOSED_EXACT + SOURCE_BLOCKED` means the experiment is operationally dead while the economic hypothesis remains unevaluated.
- `CLOSED_EXACT + NO_EDGE/TIER_4` means the exact candidate is scientifically terminal.
- A broad edge family does not die merely because one implementation failed.

## 4. Exact-experiment death

An exact experiment is permanently terminal when its frozen decision rule says stop and the canonical run produces a valid terminal scientific classification.

Examples include adequate frozen Discovery/OOS/holdout evidence that produces `NO_EDGE`, `TIER_4_REJECTED`, wrong sign, materially negative frozen-cost economics, or another prospectively declared terminal failure.

After exact-experiment death:
- the same LAB_ID/MVE cannot be rerun to seek a different answer;
- no favorable asset, year, quarter, direction, horizon, threshold or cost may be isolated;
- no failed primary may be replaced by a winning secondary diagnostic;
- no new source may be used merely to overwrite a valid scientific result;
- unused protected periods are not an automatic rescue resource.

The evidence remains queryable and must be carried into anti-duplication checks.

## 5. Non-scientific terminal closure

A lab may be operationally closed without a scientific verdict when the blocker is source, provenance, data integrity, sample adequacy or technical feasibility.

Such a lab receives:
- exact experiment state: `CLOSED_EXACT` or `DORMANT_TRIGGERED`;
- scientific state: the original non-scientific classification;
- mechanism state: normally `UNRESOLVED`;
- an explicit reopening trigger, if any;
- explicit forbidden substitutions.

This is the anti-zombie rule. A blocked lab is not allowed to consume endless source-search cycles. Once the bounded source plan is exhausted, it sleeps until a pre-defined external trigger occurs.

## 6. Reopening a blocked exact experiment

An exact blocked experiment may reopen only if all of the following are true:

1. Its closeout explicitly permits reopening or the blocker was a purely pre-outcome technical/source condition.
2. The triggering input is exogenous to observed market outcomes: e.g. provider archive repair, newly available canonical public source, previously authorized entitlement, recovered byte-exact source, or credentials to the already-frozen source.
3. Signal, universe, clock, direction, thresholds, costs, sample gates and classification rules are unchanged.
4. The reopening source passes the original evidence standard.
5. No easier event/sample/source is substituted for a frozen validation challenge.
6. A new immutable reopening receipt is written before outcome access.

If the measurement definition, information set or economic contract changes, it is not a reopening. It is a new experiment with a new LAB_ID.

## 7. Pre-outcome supersession

A technical implementation may be superseded under the same scientific identity only when the failure happened before relevant outcomes were opened and the correction is demonstrably non-scientific: environment, transport, literal/schema bug, deterministic decoder defect, packaging or equivalent plumbing.

The amendment must prove:
- outcomes remained unopened;
- the scientific contract is byte-/field-equivalent except for the technical correction;
- the prior implementation is marked `SUPERSEDED_PRE_OUTCOME`;
- hashes and exact diffs are preserved.

Once outcomes have informed the correction, same-ID supersession is forbidden. Use a new prospective experiment.

## 8. Mechanism continuation gate

Failure of an exact experiment does not automatically kill the broad economic mechanism. However, a successor is legitimate only if it passes all of the following before new outcomes:

### 8.1 Material causal novelty
The successor must add or change a causal economic state, participant constraint, information source, market structure or payoff mechanism. A different parameter, asset, venue, timeframe or horizon is not enough.

### 8.2 Independent rationale
The new mechanism must be justified without selecting a feature because it looked favorable in the failed experiment's outcomes. Acceptable rationale can come from pre-existing theory, protocol mechanics, market rules, externally documented structure, or a prospectively collected information source.

### 8.3 Contamination map
The successor must list every historical block, diagnostic and outcome that was visible during design. Those observations cannot be represented as independent validation.

### 8.4 New immutable identity
A materially changed mechanism uses a new LAB_ID, new freeze, new source authority, new failure mode and new validation path.

### 8.5 Zero inherited promotion credit
Positive evidence from the parent may motivate research but cannot promote the successor. Negative evidence from the parent must remain disclosed.

### 8.6 Fresh independent evidence
The successor's decisive evidence must come from a genuinely untouched block or prospective post-freeze observations. The design cannot consume protected data merely to decide whether the successor deserves to exist.

If these conditions do not pass, the proposal is a rescue/rebrand and is rejected before outcomes.

## 9. Family exhaustion

A broad family may be marked `EXHAUSTED_WITHIN_DEFINED_SCOPE` only when the scope itself was prospectively defined and multiple materially independent implementations directly test the same causal proposition with adequate evidence.

"Trend", "mean reversion", "flow", "volatility", "credit", "event" or another broad registry family can never be declared dead from one failed strategy.

V4 therefore distinguishes:
- dead exact implementation;
- dead exact mechanism formulation;
- still-open broader family.

## 10. Archive policy

Archival is evidence preservation, not deletion.

A branch/reference is safe to archive only when:
- the canonical authority and closeout are independently reachable;
- hashes/receipts are preserved;
- no active workflow depends on that ref;
- its scientific state is already explicit;
- archiving cannot obscure a blocker or convert it into a stone.

No branch deletion is authorized by V4. No main merge is authorized by V4.

Blocked, insufficient, provenance-failed and data-failed labs may be archived operationally only if the archive index preserves their non-scientific status and reopening trigger.

## 11. Mandatory tombstone for every closed lab

Every terminal or dormant lab must record:
- LAB_ID / MVE_ID;
- parent mechanism / family;
- frozen authority;
- canonical execution and evidence hash;
- exact scientific classification;
- whether outcomes were opened;
- exact reason for closure;
- forbidden rescues;
- protected data still unopened;
- reopening trigger, or `NONE`;
- whether a materially new successor is scientifically permissible;
- successor contamination boundary.

This tombstone is the first anti-duplication source for future exploration.

## 12. Anti-hindsight firewall

Before any new experiment:
- source and event identity are fixed;
- outcome-sensitive fields remain sealed until the source gate passes;
- protected periods remain protected;
- source selection cannot use performance;
- missing values stay missing;
- current/revised records cannot impersonate point-in-time data;
- diagnostics from a failed run cannot choose the next threshold or subgroup;
- a new candidate must be screened against terminal, blocked and active lineages by mechanism, not branch name.

## 13. Exploration policy

New work should prioritize materially independent information families and mechanisms with defensible point-in-time sources.

Over-covered or exhausted transformations must not be rebranded as novelty. In particular, generic indicator/timeframe permutations, generic lead-lag permutations, generic carry threshold mutations and post-hoc subgroup selection require a materially new causal information channel before they can become new labs.

A source-feasibility pass is not an edge signal. It only gives permission to freeze and test the mechanism.

## 14. V4 relationship to Promotion Policy V3

V4 governs whether a lab may exist, continue, sleep or die.

Promotion Policy V3 governs the tier evidence required after a valid candidate exists.

V4 cannot promote a candidate. V3 cannot revive a V4-terminal exact experiment by changing labels. Both preserve all prior historical verdicts.

## 15. Safety and execution boundary

Research-only by default.

This policy authorizes no live trading, wallet action, order, leverage, exchange mutation, paid-data purchase, trial, identity submission, deployment, protected-holdout opening, production capital or merge to main.

## 16. Frozen decision rule

When uncertain between "dead" and "unresolved", choose the state supported by the canonical evidence:
- valid economic contradiction -> terminal exact scientific death;
- source/data/provenance/technical/sample blocker -> non-scientific closure;
- historical archive only -> no inference;
- materially new causal hypothesis -> new LAB_ID, never resurrection.

The burden of proof is on continuation, not on closure.
