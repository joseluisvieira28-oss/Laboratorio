# SOURCE ADEQUACY POLICY — PROSPECTIVE AMENDMENT V0.1

Date: 2026-09-19

## Purpose

Avoid throwing away potentially valuable research sources merely because a source-coverage threshold is not met, while preserving anti-hindsight discipline.

## Rule from this point forward

A source gate and a promotion gate are different things.

For newly opened source lanes, coverage is classified as:

- **SOURCE_FULL** — satisfies the predeclared coverage target.
- **SOURCE_LIMITED** — real, structurally useful data exists, but breadth/depth is below the preferred target.
- **SOURCE_BLOCKED** — the required data fields/provenance are absent or inaccessible.

A SOURCE_LIMITED result may support a separately frozen **exploratory, non-promotional** study when:
1. the economic rule is frozen before outcomes;
2. uncertainty is reported explicitly;
3. no claim of production readiness or diamond promotion is made from the limited sample alone;
4. a later independent replication/OOS gate is still mandatory.

## Anti-rescue boundary

This amendment is prospective. It does **not** rewrite:
- the Cryptarbitrage 90-day source gate;
- the Binance short-vol Discovery;
- any closed MVE or historical verdict.

The old 68-day Cryptarbitrage result remains what it was under its frozen gate. Future mines will not be discarded solely because a preferred source breadth target is missed; they will be labeled SOURCE_LIMITED rather than conflated with NO_EDGE.

## Scientific intent

Be strict on promotion, not needlessly destructive at source reconnaissance.
