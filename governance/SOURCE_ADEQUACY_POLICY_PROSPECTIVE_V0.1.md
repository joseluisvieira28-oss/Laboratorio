# SOURCE ADEQUACY POLICY — PROSPECTIVE AMENDMENT V0.1

Date: 2026-09-19

For newly opened source lanes, distinguish:
- SOURCE_FULL — preferred coverage target met.
- SOURCE_LIMITED — real and structurally useful source exists, but breadth/depth is below the preferred target.
- SOURCE_BLOCKED — required fields/provenance/access are absent.

SOURCE_LIMITED may support a separately frozen exploratory/non-promotional study. Promotion still requires independent replication/OOS and stronger evidence.

This is prospective only. It does not rewrite any already-opened or closed gate, including the 68-day Cryptarbitrage result, the Binance short-vol Discovery, or any historical MVE.
