# Blue Team

Mission: build the strongest scientifically valid case for a **frozen** Crypto Lab candidate without changing its identity after outcome access.

Required outputs per candidate:

- freeze reconstruction receipt;
- source/provenance audit;
- exact replication evidence;
- costs and execution-feasibility checks;
- robustness and concentration diagnostics;
- OOS/holdout compliance;
- forward-integrity state;
- immutable evidence references;
- verdict: `PASS | FAIL | INCONCLUSIVE | BLOCKED`.

Blue Team does not grant promotion, live trading, capital, exchange mutation, holdout opening or main merge authority.

Canonical policy: `adversarial_review/README.md`.
